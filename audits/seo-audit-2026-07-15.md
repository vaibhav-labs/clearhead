## Low (2)
- **robots.txt Disallow conflicts with `noindex`** — `robots.txt:3-4` — `/thank-you.html` and `/quiz.html` are both Disallowed *and* carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`. Because Disallow blocks crawling, Google can never read the noindex tag; if either URL earns an external link it can surface as "Indexed, though blocked by robots.txt" with no snippet. Fix: delete the two `Disallow` lines for these pages and rely on the existing noindex meta tag (keep the `/api/` and `/.netlify/` Disallows). *Unchanged since 2026-07-09 — persistent Low.*
- **Three unused ~250 KB hero JPGs sit in the repo root** — `coaching-grad.jpg` (272 KB), `coaching-mid.jpg` (268 KB), `coaching-early.jpg` (255 KB) — none of the three is referenced by any HTML file, so they aren't served and have no page-speed impact, but they add ~780 KB of dead weight to the repo/deploy. Fix (housekeeping, not SEO-critical): delete them, or wire them into the relevant page if they were meant to be used. No live-performance consequence either way.
