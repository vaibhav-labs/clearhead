5. Coaching agreement

Please read it fully. Scroll the box below. This agreement sits alongside the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy; where they differ, this signed agreement applies.

1. Parties

This agreement is between Vaibhav Jain, ICF PCC, trading as Clearhead, Lokhandwala Complex, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053 ("Coach"), and the person who signs below ("Client").

2. What this is, and what it is not

  • Coaching and counselling here is a confidential, structured, one-to-one conversation to help working professionals think through decisions, career, identity and wellbeing.
  • It is not therapy, psychiatry, psychology or medical treatment. The Coach does not diagnose or treat any mental health condition and is not a licensed therapist or medical practitioner.
  • It is not a crisis service and is not available 24/7. If you are in crisis or having thoughts of harming yourself, contact Tele-MANAS 14416 / 1800-89-14416, emergency 112, Vandrevala Foundation 1860-2662-345 or iCall 9152987821, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
  • No outcome is guaranteed. The Client remains responsible for their own decisions and actions.

3. Package and fees

The Client has chosen the package selected above. Sessions are 60 minutes, one-to-one, online or in person in Lokhandwala, Andheri West. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

Payment is made in full, in a single payment, before the first session. Part payments and instalments are not offered. Payments are processed through Razorpay; Clearhead does not store card or bank details. WhatsApp support between sessions is for brief logistics and short check-ins, not for in-depth coaching or emergencies.

4. Scheduling

  • Sessions are booked through Cal.com.
  • Reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice. Less than 24 hours' notice, or a no-show, counts as a used session with no makeup.
  • If you arrive late, the session still ends at the scheduled time.
  • If the Coach has to cancel at short notice (illness, emergency, connectivity), a free makeup session is offered and nothing is deducted from your package.
  • A package may be paused once for up to 30 days, by notifying the Coach before the next scheduled session. Pausing does not entitle you to a refund.

5. Refunds

All sales are final. If you cancel before the second session is completed, you will be refunded in full. After the second session is completed, no refunds are given for unused sessions, change of mind, dissatisfaction, scheduling conflicts or any other reason.

6. Confidentiality

  • Nothing from our sessions is disclosed to anyone without your explicit written consent.
  • Limited exceptions: (a) a clear and imminent risk of serious harm to you or another identifiable person; (b) disclosure compelled by valid legal process from a court of competent jurisdiction in India.
  • The Coach may keep brief session notes and may discuss work anonymously, with no identifying details, in professional supervision, as part of ICF ethical practice. Your data is handled as set out in the Privacy Policy.
  • You agree to keep the Coach's frameworks and materials for your personal use only.

7. Client commitments

  • I am 18 or older and the information I have given is accurate.
  • I understand this is coaching and counselling, not clinical or emergency care, and I will tell the Coach if my circumstances change, for example if I begin treatment for a mental health condition.
  • I will engage in good faith and take responsibility for my own decisions.
  • I will not record, transcribe or share the content of sessions without the Coach's written consent.

8. Liability and governing law

To the extent permitted by law, the Coach's total liability is limited to the amount paid for the package in question, and the Coach is not liable for indirect or consequential loss or for decisions made by the Client. This agreement is governed by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Mumbai, Maharashtra.

9. Ending the work

Either party may end the coaching relationship at any time by telling the other. Refunds, if any, follow section 5. If the Coach believes coaching is no longer the right service, for example because clinical care would serve you better, the Coach will say so and suggest appropriate referrals.
